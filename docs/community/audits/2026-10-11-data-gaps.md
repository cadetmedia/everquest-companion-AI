- **Do not re-derive items.json or mobs.json from this clone's caches**: they are older than the committed data (section 3 would become 892 + 124 regressions). Refresh the caches first (the delta only patches the cache in the checkout it runs in, and this clone's was skipped), or run the full scrapes above; then the two provable parser fixes (27 templated summaries, 4 elemental DMG stats) land as a side effect.

Parser-side / key-side (no requests at all)
- Loot parser: strip ` from Reward Chest` from `loot.item` (67 names, 66 already in items.json); read the `You looted # X from Reward Chest and stored it in your tradeskill depot/currency` and `...sold it for free` lines (126 lines, currently `unknown`).
- `itemKey` / lookup: strip ` (Exaltation)` (13 names); resolve `Spell: X`/`Song: X` (477 log names, 192 mob-drop names) to spells.json; label `itemMerge.item` as a spell rank, not an item (76 names).

- Mob lookup: article-insensitive fallback against the catalog (19 names, including the 1,412-cast `a fire giant warrior`).
- `zoneKey`: strip a trailing instance number from `/who` zone strings (21 names) and alias `Temple of Cazic-Thule` -> `cazicthule` (1 name, 29 events).
- `parseStatsBlock`: the glued `HP Regen: 2 Mana Regen: 2 End Regen: 2` line (1 item) and the four other glued values above; another agent owns this.
- Engine parser candidates by volume: faction standing lines (39,909), DoT `You have taken # damage from X by Y.` (15,718), ability cooldowns (108,968), `X's <spell> spell is interrupted.` (2,470).
